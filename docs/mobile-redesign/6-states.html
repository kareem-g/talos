34 · KEYBOARD OPEN

Composer rides the keyboard

The composer is keyboard-avoiding: it lifts with the keyboard rather than being covered, the config chips compress to two, and the transcript shrinks rather than scrolling behind the keys. Slash menus render above the field, never under the keyboard.
